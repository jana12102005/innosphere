About Paavai Innovation Forum

Paavai Innovation Forum is an institutional initiative of Paavai Educational Institutions, established to promote innovation, research, and problem-solving culture among students. The forum functions as a central platform that encourages students to explore real-world challenges through technology, research, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

The forum operates under the visionary leadership of Prof. Dr. R. R. Krishnamurthy, Director – Centre for Research, Paavai Educational Institutions, and Ms. Kamala Krishnamurthy, Centre Head – Paavai Innovation Forum. Their guidance and forward-thinking approach laid the foundation for a structured and impactful innovation ecosystem.

Formation of InnoSphere Club

The formation of the InnoSphere Club was proposed and established by Janarthanan P. Recognizing the immense potential of students and their capability to solve complex real-world problems, students who had exposure to national-level internships at premier institutions such as IISc and other reputed research centers were brought together to form a strong core team under the Paavai Innovation Forum.

This collective effort led to the establishment of InnoSphere Club — an innovation-driven, interdisciplinary student organization. The name “InnoSphere” represents an Innovation Sphere, where ideas, creativity, and technology intersect to create impact.

Vision

To build a sustainable, innovation-centric ecosystem that empowers students to transform ideas into impactful solutions for real-world problems.

Mission

  • To encourage interdisciplinary collaboration among students from all departments.
  • To bridge the gap between academic knowledge and real-world applications.
  • To promote research, innovation, and hands-on problem solving.
  • To prepare students for industry, research, and entrepreneurial challenges.

Objectives of InnoSphere

  • Identify real-world problems and develop practical, scalable solutions.
  • Create student teams capable of handling both smart work and complex technical challenges.
  • Provide exposure to research, internships, competitions, and innovation programs.
  • Encourage innovation across domains such as technology, healthcare, environment, and society.

Organizational Structure

The InnoSphere Club functions under the guidance of the Paavai Innovation Forum and follows a structured hierarchy:

Patron → Director → Centre Head → Faculty Coordinators → President → Secretary → Event Coordinators → Research Leads → Student Members

Note: All student coordinators and office bearers function under the guidance of the Centre Head and Director to ensure quality, discipline, and impactful outcomes.

Our Philosophy

InnoSphere believes that students possess immense untapped potential. With the right mentorship, collaborative environment, and exposure, students can contribute meaningfully to innovation and societal development. The club stands as a platform where ideas are nurtured, transformed, and executed effectively.
